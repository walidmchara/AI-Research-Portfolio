## 🧠 Research Approach

My research frequently combines multiple complementary learning paradigms:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Signal Processing / Wavelet Decomposition
   ↓
Feature Engineering
   ↓
Deep Feature Extraction
   ↓
CNN / RNN / Transformer
   ↓
Attention Mechanism
   ↓
Residual Learning
   ↓
Optimization
   ↓
Prediction / Diagnosis
   ↓
Explainability & Evaluation
```

The goal is to build models that are not only accurate but also robust, generalizable, computationally efficient, and suitable for real-world engineering systems.
